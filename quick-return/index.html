Equations and sign conventions

Lever pivot O₄ at the origin, crank pivot O₂ a distance d directly above it. The ram C slides on the line y = h. Angles are measured counter-clockwise from +x; positive ω and α are counter-clockwise; positive ram velocity is to the right. s is the distance O₄A along the lever.

Position (loop O₄ → O₂ → A)

r·eiθ₂ + i·d = s·eiθ₄
s = √[(r cos θ₂)² + (d + r sin θ₂)²],  θ₄ = atan2(d + r sin θ₂, r cos θ₂)

Velocity, with δ = θ₂ − θ₄

ṡ = −r ω₂ sin δ,  ω₄ = r ω₂ cos δ / s

Acceleration

s̈ = s ω₄² − r (ω₂² cos δ + α₂ sin δ)
α₄ = [r (α₂ cos δ − ω₂² sin δ) − 2 ṡ ω₄] / s
Coriolis: acor = 2 ṡ ω₄, perpendicular to the lever

Ram (loop O₄ → B → C), lever length L, link length l₅

sin θ₅ = (h − L sin θ₄) / l₅,  xC = L cos θ₄ + l₅ cos θ₅
ω₅ = −L ω₄ cos θ₄ / (l₅ cos θ₅)
vC = −L ω₄ sin θ₄ − l₅ ω₅ sin θ₅
α₅ = (L ω₄² sin θ₄ − L α₄ cos θ₄ + l₅ ω₅² sin θ₅) / (l₅ cos θ₅)
aC = −L α₄ sin θ₄ − L ω₄² cos θ₄ − l₅ α₅ sin θ₅ − l₅ ω₅² cos θ₅

Quick return. The lever is at its extremes when the crank is perpendicular to it, at an angle ψ from O₂O₄ with cos ψ = r/d.

Time ratio = (360° − 2ψ) / 2ψ,  ram stroke ≈ 2 L r / d

The Coriolis direction is found by rotating the slip velocity ṡ through 90° in the sense of ω₄.
