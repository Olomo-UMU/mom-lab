Equations and sign conventions

Crank pivot O at the origin; slider B moves along y = e. Angles θ (crank) and φ (rod) are measured counter-clockwise from +x. Positive ω and α are counter-clockwise; positive slider velocity is away from O.

Loop closure

r·eiθ + l·eiφ = xB + i·e
sin φ = (e − r sin θ) / l,  xB = r cos θ + l cos φ

Velocity

ω3 = −r ω2 cos θ / (l cos φ)
vB = −r ω2 sin θ − l ω3 sin φ

Acceleration

α3 = (r ω2² sin θ − r α2 cos θ + l ω3² sin φ) / (l cos φ)
aB = −r α2 sin θ − r ω2² cos θ − l α3 sin φ − l ω3² cos φ
In-line approx. (α2 = 0): aB ≈ −r ω2² (cos θ + cos 2θ / n),  n = l/r
